    Important currency facts

    🥇

    Highest value

    The Kuwaiti Dinar (KWD) is the highest-valued currency per unit, followed by the Bahraini Dinar and Omani Rial.

    🌍

    Reserve currency

    The US Dollar (USD) is the world's main reserve currency and is used in most international trade.

    💶

    Euro zone

    The Euro (EUR) is shared by many European countries, so one currency covers many flags.

    📈

    Biggest market

    The foreign exchange market is the largest financial market, trading over $7 trillion every day.

    🇮🇳

    Rupee symbol

    The Indian Rupee symbol ₹ was officially adopted in 2010.

    💷

    Oldest currency

    The British Pound is the oldest currency still in use, with a history of over 1,000 years.

    🏔️

    Safe haven

    The Swiss Franc (CHF) is seen as a safe-haven currency during global uncertainty.

    🔗

    Pegged currencies

    Gulf currencies like AED, SAR, QAR, BHD and OMR are pegged to the US Dollar.

    🪙

    Shared currency

    Countries like Ecuador and El Salvador use the US Dollar as their official money.

    📉

    Lowest per unit

    Currencies such as the Iranian Rial, Vietnamese Dong and Lao Kip have very small value per unit.

    ⏱️

    Always changing

    Exchange rates move every second based on demand, supply, interest rates and news.

    🏦

    Central banks

    Central banks like the RBI, Fed and ECB influence currency strength through interest rates.
